## 1. Why are Overseas Brands Shifting from "Text Monitoring" to "Visual Intelligence"?

In the past, when cracking down on counterfeit goods or unauthorized sales, brands often relied on text keywords for retrieval (for example, searching for their brand name). But the reality is that many non-compliant merchants, to evade review, will use homophones or garbled text in product titles and descriptions, while directly stealing official high-definition images of the brand in their main pictures.

If relying solely on text monitoring, brands will become completely "blind." Therefore, visual intelligence analysis has become the core of modern brand protection:

* **Discovering hidden image theft:** Automatically identifying whether unauthorized use of official product images, model photos, or promotional posters occurs on public e-commerce platforms and social platforms across the web.
* **Preventing "copycat" appearances:** Using AI vision models to compare the public appearance displays of products, promptly discovering highly similar counterfeit products, and providing clues for legal evidence collection.
* **Competitor visual strategy analysis:** Observing the public ad images and main picture designs that competitors place in different national markets to analyze their localized marketing strategies.

## 2. How Do AI Vision Models Reshape "Public Data Aggregation"?

To achieve the above goals, enterprises cannot rely on human eyes to inspect the entire web. Modern AI vision systems achieve compliant visual intelligence aggregation through the following steps:

* **Multimodal feature extraction:** AI can not only extract text from images (OCR) but also identify objects, scenes, and even the feature vectors of brand logos within the image.
* **Mass comparison (Data Matching):** The system rapidly compares the massive public image features aggregated back with the benchmark of the enterprise's own image library. Once the similarity exceeds the alert threshold, the system immediately generates an infringement clue report for the legal team to conduct manual review.
* **Cross-regional visual verification:** The official display materials of the same brand in different countries (such as the US vs. Germany) may differ. AI needs to be able to pull public pages in specific target markets for localized visual comparison analysis.
